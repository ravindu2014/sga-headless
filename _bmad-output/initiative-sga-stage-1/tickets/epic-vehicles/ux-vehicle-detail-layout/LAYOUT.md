# Vehicle detail — where each block sits

Measured on 25 Nov 2026 at [2014 Nissan (R35) GT-R Premium Edition](https://collectingcars.com/for-sale/2014-nissan-r35-gt-r-4). Desktop is 1440×900. Phone is 390×844.

This note places blocks we already show. It does not add auction chrome, and it does not change a block’s size.

## Desktop

Two columns. The title is not a full-width band above them. The right column starts level with the top of the photo.

| Column | Top to bottom |
|--------|----------------|
| Left | Main photo, gallery thumbnails, description, features |
| Right | Availability and featured badges, title, year · make · model, price, specifications, WhatsApp enquiry |

On the reference page the photo column starts near x=72 and the title starts near x=868, y=243, level with the photo. The price sits under the title in that right column (about y=537). Specification labels start under the price (about y=948), still in the right column, in two cells per row. WhatsApp sits under the specifications. The written description starts under the gallery, in the left column (about y=1446), so it sits beside the lower part of the right column rather than under the whole page.

## Phone

One column. The photo comes first (about y=220). The title comes after it (about y=540). Then the same right-column order: badges, title, year · make · model, price, specifications, WhatsApp enquiry. Description and features follow that stack.

## Kept as they are

Photo ratio, title size, specification cell padding, price size, enquiry control sizes, and the enquiry’s sticky offset under the header. The price is not inside the enquiry box; the enquiry stays the sticky piece.

## Not on our page

Place Bid, countdown, bid history, photo category tabs, save, share, VIN, seller, and location. Those stay off this page.
